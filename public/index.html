Sample Case Study

The Rustic Fork

Fictional restaurant used for demonstration purposes. Service: Get Found & Get Seen.

The situation

The Rustic Fork had a Google listing with outdated hours, no menu link, three unanswered 1-star reviews, and only 4 photos, none of them professional. Their Instagram hadn't posted in 6 weeks.

What was done

  • Rebuilt the Google Business Profile: corrected hours, added a menu link, added 9 new photos (interior, plated dishes, exterior signage)
  • Wrote and posted professional responses to all outstanding reviews
  • Added a Google Post announcing a weekend special
  • Delivered 4 social posts over 2 weeks: 2 food-focused, 1 behind-the-scenes kitchen shot, and 1 "meet the owner" post, each with a written caption
  • Provided a simple content calendar for the following 2 weeks

The result (illustrative, not a guarantee)

A restaurant in this situation typically sees more profile views, more direction requests, and better first impressions from search, since incomplete or outdated Google listings are one of the biggest silent conversion killers for local businesses.
